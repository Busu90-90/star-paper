Our promises

  • Your records are never held back. If you stop paying, your account becomes read-only. You can still open and export everything. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it.
  • Founding price for life. Managers who join during early access keep the price of their plan for as long as they stay subscribed.
  • No surprise charges. You see the price before you pay, and you can cancel any time in Settings.
  • Pay the way you already pay. We plan to accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money and cards.

Questions

What counts as an artist? Each artist profile in your Artists page. Bookings, payments and expenses have no limit on any plan.

Can I change plans? Yes, up or down at any time. If you move down and have more artists than the plan allows, nothing is deleted; you just can’t add new ones until you are within the limit.

Is my data safe if I leave? Yes. You can export everything, or delete your account yourself in Settings, Account, Delete my account.
