Plans in Uganda shillings

Every plan has every feature. The first 30 days are free, with no payment details needed.

Every plan includes

Bookings, deposits and payments with no limit. Statements for each artist. The documents vault for contracts and IDs. Payment follow-ups and money forecasts. Crew chat. Export of all your data at any time.

Bring a manager, earn a month

  • Your friend gets 30 extra free days when they join through your link, add an artist and log a booking.
  • You get a free month on your plan when they make their first payment.
  • Free for life: once 5 managers you brought are paying at the same time, you pay nothing for as long as they keep paying.

Our promises

  • Your records are never held back. If you stop paying, your account becomes read-only. You can still open and export everything, forever. Nothing is deleted unless you delete it.
  • Founding price for life. Managers who pay during early access keep the price of their plan for as long as they stay subscribed.
  • No surprise charges. You see the price before you pay, and you can cancel any time in Settings.
  • Pay the way you already pay. We plan to accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money and cards.

Questions

What counts as an artist? Each artist profile in your Artists page. Bookings, payments and expenses have no limit on any plan.

What counts as a team member? Each person you invite to your team, such as a road manager, assistant or accountant. They don’t pay anything themselves.

Can I change plans? Yes, up or down at any time. If you move down and have more artists or team members than the plan allows, nothing is deleted; you just can’t add new ones until you are within the limit.

What happens after the free 30 days? Pick a plan to keep adding shows and payments. If you don’t, your account becomes read-only and everything stays there for when you come back.

Is my data safe if I leave? Yes. You can export everything, or delete your account yourself in Settings, Account, Delete my account.
