Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Star Paper is a workspace for artist managers, labels, booking agents and tour crews. You trust us with your bookings, money and team details, so this page explains in plain words what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. Star Paper is run from Kampala, Uganda, and we handle personal data in line with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address and password when you sign up, and a phone number if you choose to add one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you put into the app. Artists and their contacts and photos, bookings, promoters, venues and dates, income, expenses, payments, budgets and goals, tasks, documents you upload, crew chat messages, and the teams you create or join.
  • Technical information. When something breaks, an error report with the page, browser, device type and what went wrong, so we can fix it. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics that follow you around the web.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your data on every device you sign in on.
  • To share data with the team members you invite, at the access level you choose.
  • To send emails you need, such as sign-up codes and password resets.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your bookings or finances for advertising.

Who helps us run Star Paper

We use a small number of service providers. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase stores your account, app data and uploaded documents, in a data centre in Ireland (EU).
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Sentry receives error reports, stored in the EU.
  • Netlify or Cloudflare host the website and deliver it to your browser.
  • Open-Meteo receives the town or venue name of a booking to show the weather forecast. It does not receive your name or account details.

Because these providers are outside Uganda, your data is transferred abroad. We choose providers that protect data to a standard at least as strong as Ugandan law requires.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and rights of our users or the service.

Your team and your data

When you invite people to a team, they can see the data you share with that team. Team owners decide who joins and what each member can change. If you join someone else’s team, the team owner can see what you add there.

On your device

Star Paper keeps a copy of the app and some of your data in your browser so it opens fast and works offline. It also remembers your sign-in and settings such as light or dark mode. Signing out clears your session. On a shared device, always sign out when you finish.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and app data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Backups roll off on their own schedule after that.

Security

Data is encrypted on its way to and from our servers and while stored. Each account and team can only reach its own records, enforced by the database itself. Passwords are checked against known breaches at sign-up. No system is perfect, so please use a strong password that you don’t use anywhere else.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. You can also object to how we use it. Email us at support@starpaper.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office.

Children

Star Paper is for working professionals and is not meant for anyone under 18. If you manage an artist who is under 18, please only add the details you need and make sure you have their guardian’s consent.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
